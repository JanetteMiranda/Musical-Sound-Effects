SOUND DIRECTOR — PORTABLE USB VERSION

1. Extract this ZIP (do not open index.html from inside the ZIP).
2. Copy the entire extracted folder to a USB flash drive.
3. On Windows or Mac, open the folder and double-click index.html.
4. Use a current browser such as Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari.
5. Click a sound button or press A through P; S stops audio.
6. The script edition also supports Left/Right arrows for line navigation.

NO INTERNET, NETLIFY, INSTALLATION OR OFFLINE CACHING REQUIRED.
Keep the audio folder next to index.html. Do not rename audio files.
If your browser restricts local audio, try Chrome or Edge.

Note: Browser audio playback may require an initial click.
